HABITLINE
=========

A minimal, local all-in-one health tracker with:

- A Home dashboard with today's habits, nutrition, plan, lifting and insights
- Checkbox and numeric habits
- Daily targets (including values such as 20k)
- Clear below-target and target-met states
- A 14-day graph
- Weekly and monthly averages
- Custom habit colours
- Date navigation for filling in previous days
- A weightlifting tracker with reusable exercise names
- Editable and deletable exercises and workout entries
- Weight, sets, reps, notes, and dated workout history
- Previous-session details while logging an exercise
- Monthly and yearly weight or training-volume progression
- Persistent light and dark modes
- A date-specific food log with amounts in grams
- Automatic macro and micronutrient estimates from per-100g food data
- Daily percentage bars for calories, protein, fat, carbs, fibre, vitamins and minerals
- Editable daily nutrition goals
- Built-in foods including cooked steak, plus editable custom foods
- Detailed targets for general nutrition, carbohydrate types, fatty acids,
  amino acids, vitamins, and minerals
- Overall target-completion and vitamin/mineral coverage score rings
- Highlighted nutrient percentage rings and grouped target tables
- No-target support for nutrients that should be recorded without a goal
- Bundled offline search for common foods with no API key required
- Optional USDA FoodData Central search for additional and branded foods
- USDA-first barcode lookup with Open Food Facts fallback
- Optional camera barcode scanning with manual barcode entry as a fallback
- Imported products saved locally and editable after import
- Reusable workout-day templates such as Push, Pull, or Legs
- Full-day logging prefilled from each exercise's previous workout
- Personal records and estimated one-rep max
- Warm-up set and barbell plate calculators
- A built-in rest timer and muscle-group training volume
- Body-weight, body-fat and measurement tracking with progress photos
- Sleep, energy, soreness, stress and mood recovery check-ins in the Body section
- Reusable meals and recipes with one-click logging
- Repeat-yesterday food logging and a recipe-powered shopping list
- A monthly planner for workouts, meals, habits, notes and reminders
- Global search across habits, exercises, foods, meals and planner items
- Smart daily suggestions based on habits, food, training and recovery
- Installable app support and an offline interface cache
- Responsive phone and desktop layouts
- Protected live PC/phone sync on the same Wi-Fi
- Age-and-bodyweight relative strength tiers
- Front/back muscle heat map based on four-week training volume
- Recurring C25K, work, kickboxing, Push/Pull and rest schedule
- Editable XP rewards and a level reset that keeps tracking history
- Kickboxing audio call-outs with repeats, rest time and speech speed
- Grade-based kickboxing XP for every completed audio repetition
- Planner completion does not award XP
- Tap-to-load spoken practice for official syllabus combinations
- Automatic local saving

START
-----

Double-click "Launch Habitline.bat". Habitline opens in your browser and
runs privately on your computer. The background process closes automatically
about 45 seconds after all Habitline tabs are closed.

The Home screen and phone Quick Add button keep common logs within two or
three taps. Profile now includes age, sex, height, weight, activity, training
experience and goal. It can estimate RMR/TDEE and apply editable nutrition
targets. These are estimates, not medical advice.

PHONE SYNC
----------

1. Connect the PC and phone to the same private Wi-Fi network.
2. Keep Habitline running on the PC.
3. In Habitline select "Phone sync", copy the private link, and open it on
   the phone.
4. If the phone cannot connect, double-click "Enable Phone Access.bat" once
   and approve the Windows prompt.

Both devices use the same live data immediately. The PC must remain on and
Habitline must remain running. This is private local-network sync, not an
internet cloud account.

On iPhone, open the private link in Safari, use Share > Add to Home Screen,
then tap Add. Habitline appears as a Home Screen app without Safari controls.
See "Install Habitline on iPhone.txt" for the exact steps. A signed App Store
IPA requires macOS, Xcode and Apple code signing; the Home Screen app is the
installable iPhone copy available directly from this Windows PC.

Your data is stored locally in:
the "data" folder beside the Habitline app files.

Habit, workout and common-food search work without internet. The normal food
Search button uses Habitline's bundled local catalogue. Online USDA is
optional and the built-in DEMO_KEY has low limits. Barcode lookup checks saved
foods first, then uses USDA or Open Food Facts when internet is available.
Camera scanning depends on browser support; manual barcode entry is always
available.

Habitline is local-only and has no account or cloud server. Journal entries,
personal goals, XP, streaks, kickboxing rounds and profile settings are saved
in the same local backup and synchronized with the phone link.

https://world.openfoodfacts.org
